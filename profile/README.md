
# DataToLab

**Real-world data, advancing AI.**

DataToLab is a discovery platform for commercial AI training data. We help teams that train, fine-tune and evaluate AI models find proprietary datasets from businesses and specialist providers, and understand what they can legally and practically use.

## What you can do on DataToLab

- **Explore the catalogue** of commercial datasets, filtered by modality, language and use case: speech and audio, video and physical AI, text, image and computer vision, healthcare, finance, code, and multimodal or agent training data.
- **Request a quote** for a specific dataset directly from its page.
- **Ask us to source** a dataset that is not listed yet.
- **List your own dataset** if you are a data provider and want to reach AI teams.
